# Astoria Study Abroad - Website Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for the Astoria Study Abroad platform. This guide outlines every feature available from both the customer's perspective and the administrative management side.

---

## 🌎 1. Customer Experience (Student View)

The Astoria website is designed to be an interactive, informative, and animated portal for students seeking study abroad opportunities.

### A. Home Page
- **Visual Hero Section**: An engaging first impression with the company's core mission.
- **Conversion Tools**: Quick links to book a free consultation or contact the team.
- **Featured Destinations**: A snapshot of the most popular countries with quick access to full details.

### B. Study Destinations (Countries)
- **Interactive Country Grid**: Browse countries like Canada, UK, USA, Europe, and Asia.
- **Detailed Country Profiles**: Each profile includes:
    - Average tuition fees.
    - Key academic highlights.
    - Popular programs and universities.
    - Call-to-action for country-specific inquiries.

### C. Astoria Blogs & News
- **The Blog Feed**: A professional, animated list of the latest education news and guides.
- **Smart Reading**: Clicking "Read Full Article" takes students to a premium reading view optimized for focus and accessibility.
- **Image Integrity**: Every blog image is shown in its full resolution and natural shape, ensuring "Full Image" visibility for posters and infographics.

### D. Services & About
- **Services Guide**: Detailed breakdown of our 1-on-1 counseling, visa processing, and training programs.
- **Story of Astoria**: Learn about the company’s history, values, and successful track record in student placement.

### E. Contact & Engagement
- **Interactive Contact Form**: Students can submit their name, contact info, preferred country, and IELTS status directly to the Astoria team.
- **Direct Support**: Quick access buttons for WhatsApp, Email, and physical office locations with Google Maps integration.

---

## 🛠️ 2. Administrative Experience (Admin View)

The Admin Dashboard is a powerful, centralized hub for managing everything on the Astoria platform.

### A. Secure Authentication
- **Admin Login**: Access to management tools is restricted to authorized personnel via a dedicated login screen (`/admin`).

### B. The Blogger-Style Editor (Blogs Tab)
This tool allows admins to manage the site's content with professional-grade features:
- **Write New Posts**: A minimalist, distraction-free writing area.
- **Functional Toolbar**: Bold, Italic, Headers, Links, and Media insertion with a single click.
- **Image Upload**: Upload images directly from your device or use an external URL. Images are never cropped and are displayed at their full natural aspect ratio.
- **SEO & Organization (Sidebar)**:
    - **Labels**: Tag posts for easy categorization on the front end.
    - **Permalinks (Slugs)**: Slugs are automatically generated from your title for SEO, but you can manually edit them for specific keyword targeting.
    - **Scheduling**: Set the specific "Published at" date and time.
    - **Location**: Add a location tag to your industry news.

### C. Student Message Management (Messages Tab)
- **Centralized Inbox**: Receive and view all inquiries submitted through the website's contact forms.
- **Read/Unread Status**: Easily track which student requests have been addressed.
- **Contact Actions**: View student phone numbers and emails to initiate direct follow-ups.

### D. Study Destination Management (Countries Tab)
- **Dynamic Content**: Add new countries or update existing ones without touching code.
- **Visuals**: Upload flags/images and update tuition data or program highlights instantly.

### E. Global Site Settings (Settings Tab)
- **Real-time Updates**: Effortlessly change the phone numbers, emails, office hours, and address shown across the entire website.
- **Social Media Links**: Update WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook links to ensure students can always find your social presence.

---

> [!TIP]
> **Pro Tip for Admins**: Always use high-resolution, square or landscape images for your blog posts. The new Blogger editor will ensure they are displayed perfectly on the website without any cropping!
